# M20 Claim-Upgrade Evidence Matrix

| claim_type | current_status | minimum_future_experiment | required_metrics | required_comparison | required_repetition | required_generalization | upgrade_decision_rule | current_allowed_wording |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Structural pipeline | supported | Already met | Artifact existence + reproducibility | None | Not required | Not required | Already met | "artifact-governed pipeline exists" |
| Response-model predictive quality | not supported | Tier 1 + Tier 2 | Held-out MAE, RMSE, qualitative agreement | Baseline model comparison | 3-5 trials per command | Not required | Held-out MAE < threshold AND better than baseline | "predictive quality not evaluated" |
| Advisory risk usefulness | not demonstrated | Tier 3 + Tier 4 | Warning-to-outcome association, before/after comparison | Baseline vs advisory condition | 5+ trials per task | Not required for initial claim | Significant association between warnings and outcomes | "advisory usefulness not demonstrated" |
| Navigation outcome correlation | not demonstrated | Tier 3 | Risk-warning exposure, outcome metrics | Correlation analysis | 5+ trials per task | Not required | Statistically significant correlation | "correlation not evaluated" |
| Navigation safety improvement | prohibited | Tier 3 + Tier 4 | Collision rate, near-miss rate, success rate | Baseline vs advisory, statistical test | 5+ trials per task | Multi-session recommended | Statistically meaningful reduction in adverse events | "safety improvement not claimed" |
| Collision reduction | prohibited | Tier 3 + Tier 4 | Collision count, collision rate | Baseline vs advisory | 5+ trials per task | Multi-session recommended | Significant reduction in collision rate | "collision reduction not claimed" |
| Success-rate improvement | prohibited | Tier 3 + Tier 4 | Task success rate, failure categories | Baseline vs advisory | 5+ trials per task | Multi-session recommended | Significant improvement in success rate | "success rate improvement not claimed" |
| Calibrated uncertainty | prohibited | Repeated trials + calibration protocol | Calibrated confidence intervals, calibration error | Comparison against oracle/ground truth | 10+ trials per command for calibration | Not required | Calibration error within acceptable bounds | "not calibrated probabilities" |
| Multi-environment generalization | not supported | Tier 1 on multiple surfaces | Per-surface response metrics | Cross-surface comparison | 3+ trials per surface | 2-3 surfaces required | Consistent response across surfaces within tolerance | "generalization not evaluated" |
| Compensation readiness | out of scope | Future separate project | Compensation algorithm + validation metrics | Comparison against uncompensated baseline | Repeated per command | Multi-surface required | Separate project with validation protocol | "compensation not implemented" |
| Safe command adapter readiness | out of scope | Future separate project | Safety validation metrics + controlled trials | Baseline vs adapter | Extensive | Multi-condition required | Separate project with safety protocol | "safe command adapter not implemented" |
