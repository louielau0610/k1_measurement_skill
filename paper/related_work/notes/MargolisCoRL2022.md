## Problem addressed

Locomotion policies can fail under out-of-distribution deployment conditions.

## Method summary

Multiplicity-of-Behavior policies provide a family of possible robot behaviors for deployment-time selection/tuning.

## Assumptions

The method assumes policy training and behavior selection access.
