## Problem addressed

Off-road navigation requires planning under uncertainty about traversability and terrain interaction.

## Method summary

STEP uses uncertainty-aware traversability mapping, CVaR tail-risk assessment, and SQP-MPC planning.

## Assumptions

The method assumes sensing and online planning integration.

## Experiment setup / platform

Extreme terrain environments such as abandoned subway and lava tube are described by the CaltechAUTHORS page.

## Metrics

Risk/traversability and field traversal evidence are relevant.

## Key findings

Safe P1 summary: risk-aware traversability and tail-risk assessment are established ideas in field robotics navigation.

## Limitations

Focuses terrain risk rather than low-level velocity command response.

## Relevance to our project

Important seed for risk-map vocabulary and navigation-risk framing.

## Difference from our project

Our M16 risk layer is offline and response-model-derived, not a terrain planner.

## Safe citation claims

FanRSS2021STEP applies uncertainty-aware traversability and CVaR risk assessment for off-road navigation.

## Do-not-claim notes

Do not use STEP as evidence that our advisory labels reduce field risk.
