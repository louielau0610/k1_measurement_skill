## Problem addressed

Learning dynamic legged locomotion skills that transfer to a real quadruped.

## Method summary

Simulation-based learning and real hardware deployment for ANYmal locomotion skills.

## Assumptions

The work assumes policy training and deployment access, not a closed-source command-only interface.

## Experiment setup / platform

ANYmal real robot deployment.

## Metrics

P1 recorded only metadata-level performance context.

## Key findings

The paper is a major seed for sim-to-real legged locomotion and hardware deployment.

## Limitations

It is not a deployment-layer measurement artifact pipeline.

## Relevance to our project

Provides related-work context for legged locomotion deployment mismatch.

## Difference from our project
