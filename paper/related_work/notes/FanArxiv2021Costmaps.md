## Problem addressed

Traversability costmaps need to account for uncertainty from robot-ground interaction, sensing, localization, and out-of-distribution terrain.

## Method summary

The paper learns cost distributions and uses CVaR to derive risk-aware costmaps.

## Assumptions

Requires terrain perception and learned costmap infrastructure.

## Experiment setup / platform

The abstract describes challenging environments including caves and abandoned subway settings.

## Metrics

Tail risk, robustness to outliers, and computational performance are relevant at abstract level.

## Key findings

Safe P1 summary: costmap uncertainty and tail-risk measures can support risk-aware navigation.
