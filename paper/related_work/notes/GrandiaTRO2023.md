## Problem addressed

Legged robots need integrated perception, collision avoidance, foothold planning, and dynamic control in rough terrain.

## Method summary

The paper presents a perceptive locomotion pipeline built around nonlinear model predictive control.

## Assumptions

The method assumes access to robot dynamics, perception, and control stack.

## Experiment setup / platform

ANYmal simulation and experimental validation are described in the abstract.

## Metrics

Dynamic climbing and terrain traversal performance are relevant; P1 did not extract numerical values.

## Key findings

Safe P1 summary: planner-controller integration is a major direction in legged robot locomotion.

## Limitations
