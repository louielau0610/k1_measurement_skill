## Problem addressed

Legged robots face changing terrain, payload, wear, and hardware variation during real deployment.

## Method summary

RMA uses a base policy and adaptation module trained in simulation to adapt online from recent proprioceptive history.

## Assumptions

The method assumes policy training and online adaptation access.

## Experiment setup / platform

The arXiv abstract reports Unitree A1 deployment and varied real-world conditions.
