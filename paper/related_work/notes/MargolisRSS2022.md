## Problem addressed

High-speed quadruped locomotion over natural terrain requires robust control under terrain and dynamics variation.

## Method summary

The paper uses reinforcement learning, adaptive velocity-command curriculum, and online system identification.

## Assumptions

The method assumes controller/policy training and deployment access.

## Experiment setup / platform

MIT Mini Cheetah real outdoor locomotion is reported on the official RSS page.

## Metrics

Speed, robustness, terrain traversal, and command-curriculum context are relevant.

## Key findings

Safe P1 summary: velocity-command curricula and online system identification are relevant tools in high-speed legged locomotion.

## Limitations

Not a measurement-only black-box SDK response model.

## Relevance to our project

Strong seed for command-conditioned locomotion and online identification as prior work.

## Difference from our project

Our project cannot claim high-speed locomotion performance and does not command the robot.

## Safe citation claims

MargolisRSS2022 uses online system identification and an adaptive velocity-command curriculum for rapid quadruped locomotion.

## Do-not-claim notes
