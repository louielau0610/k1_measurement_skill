# P15 LaTeX Planning Claim Audit

## Purpose

Verify that P15 planning documents do not overclaim and preserve all scientific evidence boundaries.

## Planning claims allowed

- A LaTeX conversion plan has been documented. (planning only)
- Venue/template options have been surveyed. (informational)
- Figure rendering and table conversion plans exist. (planning only)
- A submission readiness checklist documents remaining blockers. (planning only)

## Submission claims not allowed

- The manuscript is submission-ready (not ready).
- Figures are rendered (not rendered).
- Tables are in LaTeX format (not converted).
- A PDF exists (not built).
- The paper is accepted or under review (not submitted).

## Scientific evidence gaps unchanged

All 7 evidence gaps from P14 preserved:
- No real navigation outcomes, no held-out evaluation, single robot/surface/session, sparse command grid, no calibrated uncertainty, no collision/success metrics, figures source-only.
