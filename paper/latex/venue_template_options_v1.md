# Venue/Template Options v1

| template_family | likely_use_case | benefits | constraints | figure_table_implications | bibliography_implications | current_readiness | action_needed_before_selection |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| IEEE conference (IEEEtran) | Robotics conference or workshop | Industry-standard; two-column format; widely accepted | 6-8 page limit; strict formatting; limited appendix space | 2-column requires compact figures; large tables may need landscape or appendix | IEEEtran bibliography style; numeric citation labels | Not ready (no navigation outcomes) | Author decision on venue category |
| ACM article/conference | HRI or broader computing venues | Clean single-column; flexible page counts | Different citation style; may expect user study elements | Single-column accommodates wider tables | ACM bibliography style; author-year or numeric | Not ready (HRI scope differs from this work) | Verify scope fit |
| RSS/CoRL (Robotics: Science and Systems / Conference on Robot Learning) | Top-tier robotics venues | Strong robotics community; accepts method/modeling papers | Competitive; expects strong experimental validation; 8-page RSS | Compact 2-column; figure quality critical | RSS/CoRL style; varied by year | Not ready (insufficient experiments) | Significant experimental expansion needed |
| arXiv technical report | Preprint dissemination | No page limit; flexible format; prior to or alongside conference submission | Not peer-reviewed; may need companion conference submission | Full flexibility; all figures and tables can be included | Any BibTeX style; often plain or IEEEtran | Format-ready after P16/P17; not scientifically ready | LaTeX conversion + figure rendering |
| Internal lab report / preprint | Repository-internal dissemination | No external constraints; full documentation | Not citable as peer-reviewed work | Full appendix inclusion; all audit tables can remain | Any style | Format-ready | No external requirements |
