## Guidelines

- Skeletons contain bullet-point plans and should not be read as final manuscript content.
- Drafts contain prose but remain clearly marked as drafts with known limitations.
- All sections must respect the claim boundaries in `paper/claims/m18_claim_audit.md` and `paper/claims/p3_related_work_claim_audit.md`.
- Do not claim final novelty, performance superiority, navigation safety improvement, or publication readiness.
